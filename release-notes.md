# Release Notes -- v0.2.1

> Released: 2026-10-29

The Disulfides has a logo. It stands at the top of the README, between the
badges and the title. The orchard, its data and the user guide are unchanged
from v0.2.0.

## What changed

**A logo.** `logo.svg` draws a class tree inside the orchard's two ring
roads, its leaf tufts tinted by the orchard's torsional energy ramp from
blue (relaxed) to red (strained), and under the title a disulfide,
Ca-Cb-Sg-Sg'-Cb'-Ca', on an arch of the same ramp. The Family and The Forest
now carry logos in the same style.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. The published orchard and its `web-data` release are
unchanged.
