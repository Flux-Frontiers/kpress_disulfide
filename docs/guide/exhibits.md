# The exhibits

Three exhibits stand in glades of their own at the ends of spurs off the
ring roads. **Groves** in Browse (++g++), or their markers on the map, take
you to them. Click an exhibit to open its plaque.

## The Spirit of Life

Art Techno's Spirit of Life, 10 m tall: life written in code. She holds the
double helix as a staff whose strands feed a chip at its crown, and the chip
radiates like the rising sun; in her open palm rests a glowing cell. Here,
among the disulfides, biology meets computation.

## The Spirit of Innovation

Eureka, Art Techno's Spirit of Innovation, 10 m tall and the only Spirit in
motion. She is pulling the idea from her mind and her heart, and the joy of
it carries her forward. Joining the two halves of a chip at her heart is the
symbolic act: a sunburst breaks out across her chest, and gold traces carry
it down her arms and legs.

The Spirits are two of the nine Spirits of
[Art Techno](https://github.com/Flux-Frontiers/art_techno), which does for the
computer age what Art Deco did for the machine age. Their models are
licensed CC BY-NC 4.0. From afar the orchard draws a simplified copy of
each; within 30 m it swaps in the full model.

## The Double Helix

A DNA double helix spinning on a spike, faster in the orchard's gusts: two
strands wound in a right-handed helix, as in the B-DNA that Watson and Crick
described in 1953 from Rosalind Franklin's X-ray photographs. Each rung is a
base pair, colored as in a sequencing trace.
