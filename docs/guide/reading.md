# Reading a class or bond

## A tree's card

Drive close to a tree and its card opens: the binary class and its family,
its bond count and a short summary. Click a tree to pin its card, even
in silent mode; click open ground to clear it. From the card:

- **Add to the album**, or ++e++, collects the class once you are within
  about 7 m. Farther off the button reads **Move closer** and the distance.
- **Open the reader** opens the tree's pages.

## The reader

The reader is a tree's classes as pages, from the trunk out: the binary
class, then each quadrant class, each octant class, and the sampled bonds.
++left++ and ++right++ turn the pages; ++up++ and ++down++ scroll one;
++esc++ closes it. The reader remembers the page each tree was left at.

A class page shows:

- Its id, tier, family and, where proteusPy records one, its function
  (allosteric or catalytic).
- Its bond count and share of the database.
- The consensus torsions, χ1 to χ5, with their spread.
- The mean torsional energy and Cα-Cα distance, with their spread.
- The model disulfide built from the consensus torsions (binary and octant
  classes).
- Its parent class, and its child classes, most populous first.
- For an octant class, its sampled bonds, lowest energy first.
- For a folded `-other` tip, the octant classes folded into it and their
  counts.

A bond page shows the bond itself, drawn ball-and-stick, and:

- The PDB entry, linked to its page at RCSB.
- The two cysteines and their chains.
- The structure's resolution.
- The bond's torsional energy, Cα-Cα distance and Sγ-Sγ distance.

In schematic mode (++v++), clicking a bead opens the reader on that class or
bond.

## The home disulfide

The hub's monument carries the home disulfide. On a bond page, **Make this
the home disulfide** moves the monument to that bond and gilds its tree. The
choice is saved in this browser. A `?home=` link overrides it for one visit;
see [Finding and linking](finding.md#links).

## The album

++l++, or Browse and the **Album** tab, lists the classes you have
collected: one per tree, so 32 binary classes in all. As the album fills you
rise through five ranks:

| Rank | Classes collected |
| --- | --- |
| Undergraduate | 1 |
| Research Assistant | 4, a tenth |
| Graduate Student | 8, a quarter |
| PhD Candidate | 16, half |
| Doctor of Philosophy | all 32 |

The cart carries a model of each class you have collected. The album is
saved in this browser.
