# Troubleshooting

## The orchard is slow

In settings:

1. Set **Bond complexity** to **Low** or **Medium**. **Ultra** draws every
   sampled bond and costs the most.
2. Turn off **Shadows and forest floor detail**. Shadows draw much of the
   orchard a second time.
3. Turn off **Wind and gentle cart motion**.

**Show geometry and frame rate** tells you what you gained.

## The orchard takes a long time to open

The first visit downloads the database, about 52 MB. Later visits use the
browser's cache. If the start screen stays on **Growing the orchard**,
reload the page.

## The tour is silent

Narration is off by default. Open settings and turn on **Read each grove
aloud on the ring**, then start the tour.

If it is on and you still hear nothing on an iPhone or iPad, check **Silent
Mode**. iOS mutes the browser's speech when the device is silenced. Turn
Silent Mode off in Control Center, reload the page, and tap once before
starting the tour.

## The sky is wrong for where I am

The orchard asked for your location once and you refused, so it guessed a
place from your time zone. To let it use your location, allow the site in
your browser's settings and reload. The clock in the top bar can also fix
the time of day: Dawn, Day, Dusk or Night.

## Cards keep popping up

Turn on silent mode with the bell in the top bar, or in the settings. You
can still click a tree to pin its card.

## My album or home disulfide is gone

The album, the home disulfide and the settings are saved in this browser.
A private window, another browser or another device starts fresh, and
clearing the site's data empties them.

## A `?tree=` or `?home=` link opens at the hub

The class or bond was not found, so the orchard opened at home as usual.
Check the id's tier letter (`00000b`, not `00000`), and that a `?home=`
bond is one of the sampled bonds. See [Links](finding.md#links).
