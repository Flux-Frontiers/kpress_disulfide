# Driving and the tour

## Keys

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| ++w++ ++s++ | Drive forward and back. Release to stop. |
| ++a++ ++d++ | Steer. You can turn in place. |
| Arrow keys, or drag | Look around. The view stays where you leave it. |
| ++space++ | Brake. |
| ++shift++ | Hurry. |
| ++r++ ++f++ | Raise and lower the view. |
| ++x++ | Look ahead again. |
| ++t++ | Look down on a tree whole (the one whose card is pinned, or the nearest), until ++t++ again or the cart moves. |
| ++e++ | Add the nearest tree's class to your album. |
| ++c++ | Next camera. |
| ++v++ | Schematic trees on or off. |
| ++n++ | Name plates on or off. |
| ++h++ | Home, the hub. |
| ++q++ | The guided tour on or off. |
| ++g++ ++b++ ++l++ | Browse: groves, classes, album. |
| ++esc++ | Close what is open; otherwise the settings. |
| ++question++ | About. |

## Touch and gamepad

On a phone or tablet:

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Stick, bottom left | Drive: up forward, down reverse, sideways to steer. |
| Drag anywhere else | Look around. |
| Target button | Shown while the view is turned: back to straight ahead. |
| Up and down arrows | Held, raise or lower the view. |
| Add | The nearest tree's class to your album, once you are close. |
| Three-dot menu | Time of day, silent mode, screenshot, settings, About. |

A gamepad works too: left stick to drive, right stick to look, A to add a
class to the album, LT to brake, RT to hurry.

## Cameras

++c++ steps through four views:

- **In the cart**: standing on the bed, a grove's sign ahead.
- **Behind the cart**: the road and the trees ahead. New visitors start
  here.
- **Above the cart**: looking down on the cart and the classes it carries.
- **God's eye**: the whole orchard from above the hub. Click a tree's card
  and **Fly there** to drop to it.

The cart carries a model of each class in your album, on stands in two
tiers.

## The guided tour

**Guided tour** on the start screen, or ++q++, sets the cart riding the ring
road. It stops at each grove, the named families first and the largest
first, and shows the grove's summary. Turn on **Read each grove aloud on the
ring** in the settings to hear it. Steer to hop off; ++q++ again stops the
tour.

On a desktop, **Ride the ring** at the bottom right does the same; it reads
**On the ring** while you ride. **Look ahead** appears above it while the
view is turned.
