# Getting started

Open [the orchard](https://flux-frontiers.github.io/kpress_disulfide/) in a
desktop browser, or on a phone or tablet. It needs WebGL, which every
current browser has. The first visit downloads the database, about 52 MB, so
give it a moment; the start screen says **Growing the orchard** until it is
ready.

## The start screen

The orchard grows behind the title, circling the database tree at the hub.
Three buttons:

- **Start driving** puts you in the lantern cart at the hub.
- **Guided tour** starts the cart on the ring road; it drives itself and
  stops at each grove, the named families first. See
  [The guided tour](driving.md#the-guided-tour).
- **About** describes the orchard, tonight's sky and the controls. ++question++
  opens it from anywhere.

## The first minute

1. Press **Start driving**.
2. Drive with ++w++ ++a++ ++s++ ++d++, and look around by dragging or with the
   arrow keys. On a phone, the stick at the bottom left drives and a drag
   anywhere else looks.
3. Drive up to a tree. Its card opens: the binary class, its family, its
   bond count. **Open the reader** shows the class and every class above it
   in the tree; see [Reading a class or bond](reading.md).
4. Close to a tree, ++e++ (or **Add to the album**) collects its class.
   Collect classes to rise through the album's ranks.
5. ++h++ takes you back to the hub from anywhere.

## The top bar

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Clean view | Hides the buttons, the search box and the hints; keeps the map. Press it again to bring them back. |
| Screenshot | Saves a picture of the orchard as it is now. |
| Clock | The time of day: Live (your own sky now), then Dawn, Day, Dusk and Night. |
| Bell | Silent mode: no cards pop up on their own. |
| Home | Back to the hub, ++h++. |
| Browse | The groves, every class and your album, ++g++ ++b++ ++l++. See [Finding and linking](finding.md). |
| Settings | ++esc++. See [Settings](settings.md). |
| About | ++question++. |
| Exit | Back to the start screen. |

On a phone the less-used buttons are in the three-dot menu.

The map at the top right shows the groves, the exhibits and your cart. Tap
a grove to list its classes.
